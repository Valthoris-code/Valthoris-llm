# Valthoris Third-Party Risk Management

## Purpose

This document defines the Third-Party Risk Management framework for the Valthoris platform.

Its objective is to ensure that all external providers, APIs, cloud services and technology partners are evaluated, monitored and managed according to cybersecurity, privacy and compliance requirements.

---

# Objectives

The framework aims to:

- identify third-party dependencies;
- assess security risks;
- reduce supply chain exposure;
- ensure regulatory compliance;
- monitor vendors continuously.

---

# Third-Party Categories

Examples include:

- Cloud Providers
- Identity Providers
- AI Providers
- Payment Services
- Infrastructure Providers
- Threat Intelligence Providers
- Open Source Dependencies

---

# Risk Assessment

Each third party shall be evaluated based on:

- Confidentiality Risk
- Integrity Risk
- Availability Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Legal Risk
- Operational Risk
- Supply Chain Risk

---

# Due Diligence

Before onboarding any provider:

- Security assessment
- Privacy assessment
- Compliance verification
- Contract review
- Data Processing Agreement (where applicable)

---

# Continuous Monitoring

Third parties shall be periodically reviewed for:

- security incidents;
- compliance changes;
- service availability;
- contractual obligations;
- vulnerability disclosures.

---

# Incident Handling

Security incidents involving third parties shall follow the Valthoris Incident Response Plan.

---

# Compliance

This framework aligns with:

- GDPR
- NIS2
- Cyber Resilience Act
- EU AI Act
- ENISA Recommendations
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27036
- OWASP ASVS

---

# Governance

Third-party risk reviews shall be documented and approved before production integration.
