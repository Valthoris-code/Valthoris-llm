# VALTHORIS CONSTITUTION

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL DOCUMENT

**Version:** 1.0

**Repository:** Valthoris-llm

---

# Preamble

Valthoris exists to protect people, organisations and societies against digital fraud, cybercrime and emerging technological threats.

Every technological decision, architectural choice, artificial intelligence capability and operational process shall remain aligned with this Constitution.

This document is the highest-level governance reference for the Valthoris platform.

---

# Mission

Protect the digital world through trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, advanced cybersecurity and ethical innovation.

---

# Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI-powered cybersecurity platform.

---

# Core Values

## Trust

Trust is earned through transparency, consistency and security.

---

## Privacy

Privacy is a fundamental human right.

User data shall always be protected.

---

## Security

Security is never optional.

Every component shall be designed assuming hostile environments.

---

## Ethics

Artificial Intelligence shall always assist and protect.

It shall never intentionally facilitate fraud, cybercrime or harmful activities.

---

## Transparency

Important AI decisions should be explainable whenever technically possible.

---

## Responsibility

Every architectural decision must consider long-term consequences.

---

# Technical Principles

The platform shall always prioritise:

- Security by Design
- Privacy by Design
- Secure by Default
- Zero Trust
- Least Privilege
- Defence in Depth
- Explainable AI
- Modular Architecture
- Scalability
- Auditability

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

AI systems shall:

- respect human dignity;
- support human decision-making;
- minimise bias;
- remain monitorable;
- remain accountable;
- remain legally compliant.

---

# Cybersecurity Principles

Valthoris shall actively defend against:

- Fraud
- Phishing
- Malware
- Identity Theft
- Financial Crime
- Social Engineering
- Cryptocurrency Scams
- Emerging Cyber Threats

---

# Compliance Principles

Valthoris shall remain aligned with:

- GDPR
- NIS2
- Cyber Resilience Act
- EU AI Act
- ENISA
- OWASP
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27701
- ISO/IEC 42001

---

# Development Principles

Every contributor shall:

- preserve security;
- preserve privacy;
- preserve compliance;
- document important decisions;
- avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# AI Assistant Principles

Any AI assistant contributing to this repository shall:

- read the Audit documentation;
- read the Compliance documentation;
- respect this Constitution;
- never intentionally weaken security;
- never bypass compliance;
- continue previous work instead of restarting it.

---

# Governance

This Constitution may only be modified after formal review and approval.
