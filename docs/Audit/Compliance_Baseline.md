# Valthoris Compliance Baseline

## Purpose

This document defines the compliance framework that governs the Valthoris platform.

It establishes the legal, ethical and cybersecurity principles that every future development must follow.

---

# Applicable Regulations

The Valthoris platform is developed in alignment with:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / RGPD)
- NIS2 Directive
- Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)
- European Union AI Act
- ENISA Cybersecurity Recommendations
- OWASP Top 10
- OWASP ASVS
- Secure by Design principles

---

# Security Principles

The platform must always follow:

- Privacy by Design
- Security by Design
- Least Privilege
- Zero Trust
- Defence in Depth
- Risk-based Decision Making

---

# Artificial Intelligence Governance

All AI components must:

- respect GDPR;
- be transparent;
- be explainable whenever possible;
- never intentionally facilitate fraud or cybercrime;
- comply with the European AI Act.

---

# Future AI Instructions

Any AI assistant working on Valthoris must:

- read the Audit folder before making changes;
- never reopen frozen audit findings;
- never modify compliance rules without explicit approval;
- maintain alignment with European cybersecurity legislation.
