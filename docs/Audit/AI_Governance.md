# Valthoris AI Governance

Status: ACTIVE
Version: 1.0
Repository: Valthoris-llm
Platform: Valthoris AI Cybersecurity Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines the governance principles for every Artificial Intelligence component integrated into the Valthoris platform.

It establishes the ethical, legal, technical and operational rules that every AI model, assistant, autonomous workflow and decision-support system must follow.

This document is mandatory for every current and future AI component developed under the Valthoris ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Valthoris shall become one of Europe's most trusted AI-powered cybersecurity platforms.

Artificial Intelligence is used to assist, protect and augment human decision-making, never to replace human accountability.

AI exists to protect citizens, organisations and critical infrastructure from cyber threats while respecting European values and legislation.

---

# Core Principles

Every AI component must operate according to the following principles:

- Transparency
- Explainability
- Accountability
- Human Oversight
- Fairness
- Privacy by Design
- Security by Design
- Zero Trust
- Ethical Decision Making
- Continuous Monitoring

---

# Human Oversight

Human supervision is mandatory.

AI shall never become the final legal authority regarding:

- legal liability;
- criminal accusations;
- financial decisions;
- identity verification;
- access revocation;
- sanctions.

Critical actions must always allow human review.

---

# AI Transparency

Whenever technically possible, AI-generated decisions shall include:

- confidence score;
- reasoning summary;
- evidence references;
- detected indicators;
- risk classification;
- timestamp;
- model version.

Users should always understand why a recommendation was produced.

---

# Explainability

Every AI decision should be reproducible.

Models should provide:

- behavioural analysis;
- threat intelligence references;
- anomaly explanations;
- decision justification.

Black-box behaviour should be minimised whenever practical.

---

# Privacy

All AI processing must comply with:

- GDPR
- Privacy by Design
- Data Minimisation
- Purpose Limitation

Personal information shall only be processed when strictly necessary.

---

# AI Safety

The platform shall actively prevent AI misuse.

AI must never intentionally:

- facilitate cybercrime;
- generate malware;
- bypass security controls;
- perform illegal surveillance;
- create phishing campaigns;
- impersonate individuals.

---

# Responsible AI

The platform follows the principles of Responsible Artificial Intelligence.

Every model should strive to be:

- lawful;
- ethical;
- technically robust;
- continuously monitored.

---

# European AI Act Alignment

Valthoris is designed to align with the principles of the European Union AI Act.

Special attention shall be given to:

- transparency;
- risk management;
- documentation;
- human oversight;
- cybersecurity robustness;
- post-deployment monitoring.

---

# AI Lifecycle

Every AI component should follow this lifecycle:

1. Design
2. Risk Assessment
3. Development
4. Validation
5. Security Review
6. Deployment
7. Monitoring
8. Continuous Improvement

---

# Model Versioning

Every deployed model must have:

- unique identifier;
- version number;
- deployment date;
- training dataset reference;
- rollback capability.

Historic versions should remain traceable.

---

# Auditability

Every AI interaction should be auditable.

Audit logs should include:

- timestamp;
- requesting user;
- model version;
- confidence;
- execution result;
- security context.

---

# Security

Every AI component is considered part of the cybersecurity perimeter.

Models shall follow:

- Least Privilege
- Zero Trust
- Secure Defaults
- Secure Communications
- Authentication
- Authorization

---

# Future AI Development

Any future AI assistant working on the Valthoris project shall:

- read the Audit documentation before making changes;
- preserve compliance with European legislation;
- preserve transparency;
- never fabricate technical evidence;
- never modify frozen audit findings without approval.

---

# Governance Review

This document shall be reviewed whenever:

- major legislation changes;
- AI Act requirements evolve;
- platform architecture changes significantly;
- a formal security audit recommends modifications.
